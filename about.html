About Okpella Royal Foundation

The Okpella Royal Foundation partners with local communities to preserve cultural heritage and expand access to education, health, and economic opportunity. We design projects in collaboration with local leaders so that solutions are community-led and sustainable.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower Okpella communities through education, health outreach, cultural preservation and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision: A resilient Okpella where tradition and opportunity enable every person to thrive.

History

The Foundation was established by community leaders to coordinate scholarships, health outreaches and cultural initiatives. Since founding, we have prioritized measurable projects that improve school attendance, expand access to basic health services, and create income opportunities for artisans and small businesses.

Programs & Impact

Our core programs include education support, health outreach, cultural preservation and economic empowerment. We track program outputs and outcomes and publish summaries when available; photos and recent highlights are in the Gallery.

Founder & Patron

HRM Micheal Sado

His Royal Majesty Micheal Sado serves as the Okuokpellagbe of Okpella and is the ceremonial patron of the Okpella Royal Foundation. With a long-standing commitment to community service, HRM Micheal Sado uses his position to convene local leaders, attract partners and guide the Foundation's strategic priorities.

Under his patronage the Foundation prioritises programs that deliver measurable benefits: scholarship and school-supplies distributions for children, regular health outreaches coordinated with local clinics, cultural events and apprenticeships to preserve Okpella traditions, and economic empowerment projects that combine skills training with small group grants. HRM Sado actively supports monitoring and reporting so communities and partners can see results and help refine future activities.

HRM Sado has championed community-driven approaches that build local capacity — convening stakeholders, supporting transparent selection of beneficiaries, and encouraging partnerships that link beneficiaries to local markets and services.
