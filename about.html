About Okpella Royal Foundation

The Okpella Royal Foundation partners with local communities to preserve cultural heritage and expand access to education, health, and economic opportunity. We design projects in collaboration with community leaders, ensuring that development is locally-rooted, sustainable and responsive to community needs.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower Okpella communities through education, health outreach, cultural preservation and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision: A resilient Okpella where tradition and opportunity enable every person to thrive.

History

The Foundation was established by community leaders to coordinate scholarships, health outreaches and cultural initiatives in Okpella, Etsako East Local Government Area of Edo State. Since founding, we have prioritized measurable projects that address the most pressing community needs: education access for youth, preventive health services, cultural preservation and economic empowerment for entrepreneurs and artisans.

Programs & Impact

Our core programs include education support through scholarships and school supplies distribution, regular health outreach clinics, cultural preservation initiatives, and economic empowerment through vocational training and micro-grants. We track program outputs and outcomes and publish summaries when available. Each program is designed with community input and managed transparently in partnership with local stakeholders.

HRM Micheal Sado: Patron and Visionary Leader

A Historic Coronation

His Royal Majesty Micheal Eshioronoya Olorunfemi Sado ascended the throne as the Okuokpellagbe of Okpella Kingdom in 2024-2025, following a transparent and historic selection process. In May 2025, Michael Sado was elected by the 24-member Okpella Kingmakers' Council after the deposition of the previous ruler, Lukman Akemokue, in accordance with Edo State's Customary Law of Succession. Competing against 11 other contenders from various branches of the royal house, Sado—an accomplished engineer from the Ukhumoyio branch of Afopkella—secured the highest votes in a democratic and publicly acknowledged transparent process. Following his election, he underwent the traditional installation rites at his late grandfather's palace, observing all customary protocols and spiritual preparations required by Okpella tradition. His coronation was celebrated as a momentous occasion, marking a new chapter of unity, accountability, and visionary leadership for Okpella Kingdom.

Transformative Leadership and Achievements

Since assuming the throne, HRM Sado has demonstrated exceptional commitment to the welfare of Okpella's people through strategic development initiatives, transparent governance, and compassionate community engagement. His leadership style is characterized by proactive problem-solving, cross-sector collaboration, and measurable impact. The following milestones exemplify his transformative vision:

🏗️ Infrastructure Development

Supervised major road rehabilitation, construction of healthcare facilities, installation of boreholes for clean water access, and renovation of community markets to improve commerce and quality of life.

🔒 Security & Safety

Established a dedicated Vigilante Headquarters, acquired modern surveillance drones to enhance community monitoring, and successfully negotiated the establishment of a Police Area Command in Okpella to ensure improved security and rapid emergency response.

💼 Economic Empowerment

Launched the Okpella Microfinance Bank to provide accessible credit to small business owners and traders; established the Okpella Revenue Mobilisation Committee to improve resource management and fund community projects sustainably.

📚 Education Support

Facilitated 400 scholarships for deserving Okpella youth to enable continuation of secondary and tertiary education, ensuring that poverty does not limit educational opportunity for the community's brightest minds.

💪 Employment Advocacy

Negotiated directly with major employers operating in the region, notably securing the reinstatement of 400 trailer drivers who had been disengaged by BUA Cement Company, protecting community livelihoods and jobs.

🏛️ Cultural Pride

Commissioned construction of a new Royal Palace as a symbol of unity, continuity, and pride in Okpella heritage. The palace project has united the community and signified the beginning of a new era of prosperity.

Community-Centered Governance

What distinguishes HRM Sado's leadership is his commitment to community-driven development and transparent governance. Rather than imposing top-down decisions, he has championed participatory approaches that build local ownership and capacity. He actively convenes stakeholders—youth leaders, women's groups, traders, farmers, and religious leaders—to identify priorities and co-design solutions. In scholarship and school-supply distributions, beneficiaries are selected through transparent processes that prioritize need and merit, ensuring that resources reach the most vulnerable. His humility and accessibility have made him a trusted mediator in conflicts and a champion of fair treatment for all citizens, regardless of status or wealth.

Vision for the Future

HRM Micheal Sado's overarching vision is to position Okpella as a model kingdom—distinguished by peace, prosperity, transparent governance, and sustainable development. He is committed to attracting investment while protecting community interests, preserving cultural identity while embracing progress, and ensuring that growth benefits all segments of society. His first coronation anniversary celebrations in 2025 highlighted achievements in education, infrastructure, security, and economic opportunity, while laying out an ambitious agenda for the coming years: expanded healthcare services, youth skills training programs, agricultural modernization initiatives, and continued infrastructural improvement.

Patronage of the Okpella Royal Foundation

As ceremonial patron of the Okpella Royal Foundation, HRM Sado has leveraged his position and influence to amplify the Foundation's work. He provides strategic guidance, convenes government and corporate partners, and ensures that Foundation programs align with broader community development goals. His patronage has elevated the Foundation's credibility and reach, enabling it to secure additional resources and partnerships. Together, HRM Sado and the Foundation are building an Okpella where education, health, culture, and economic opportunity are no longer luxuries but rights accessible to all.
