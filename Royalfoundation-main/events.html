Events & Activities

  1. Recent

    Backpack & School Supplies Drive

    We recently ran a backpack and school-supplies distribution for students across Okpella to support attendance and learning. The event targeted primary and secondary pupils from low-income families and included mentoring sessions for older students.

  2. Recent

    Health Outreach

    Our mobile clinics provide basic screenings, health education and referral support. These clinics are run in partnership with local health workers to ensure continuity of care.

  3. Recent

    Ramadan Food Support

    During Ramadan the Foundation coordinates community food-support activities and shared iftar gatherings for vulnerable families to ensure everyone can observe the period with dignity.

  4. Ongoing

    40 Women Grants

    An ongoing economic empowerment initiative that provides small grants and skills training to groups of women artisans. The project aims to increase household incomes and support local markets.
