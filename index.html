Welcome

The Okpella Royal Foundation supports local communities through scholarships, health outreaches, cultural preservation and economic empowerment. Our work is planned with local leaders and delivered by volunteers and partner organisations to ensure community ownership and sustainability.

  • Mission: Empower communities through education, health, and cultural preservation.
  • Vision: A thriving Okpella where tradition and progress work together.

Founder

Founder & Patron

HRM Micheal Sado serves as the Okuokpellagbe of Okpella and is the Foundation's ceremonial patron. His endorsement helps the Foundation convene partners and community leaders in support of local programs.
