Welcome

The Okpella Royal Foundation supports local communities through scholarships, health outreaches, cultural preservation and economic empowerment. Our work is planned with local leaders and focused on sustainable impact.

  • Mission: Empower communities through education, health, and cultural preservation.
  • Vision: A thriving Okpella where tradition and progress work together.

Our Impact

Empowering Lives

Through our programs, we directly support women entrepreneurs, students, and families with grants, training, and resources to build sustainable futures.

Our Programs

🎒

Backpack & School Supplies Drive

We distribute backpacks and school supplies to students to support attendance and learning.

Regular distribution schedules help ensure every student has the tools they need to succeed.

🩺

Health Outreach

Regular mobile clinics and screening events provide basic diagnostics and health education in partnership with local clinics and volunteers.

🏺

Culture & Heritage

We support cultural festivals, document local history and mentor young people in traditional arts and performances.

💼

Economic Empowerment

Vocational training, micro-grants and small-business support to help artisans and entrepreneurs grow sustainable livelihoods.

🌍

Ramadan Support

Special initiatives during Ramadan to support families with food packages, educational resources, and community gatherings.

📚

Education & Scholarships

We provide scholarships and mentoring to help talented students continue their education and reach their potential.
